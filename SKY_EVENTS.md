## Seasons (equinoxes and solstices)

These are great teaching moments: the tropical zodiac resets on these dates, the sidereal one doesn't.

- March equinox (~Mar 20): tropical "Aries season" starts, but the Sun is still in sidereal Pisces
- June solstice (~Jun 21)
- September equinox (~Sep 22)
- December solstice (~Dec 21)
