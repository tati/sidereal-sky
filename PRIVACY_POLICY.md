# Privacy Policy for Sidereal Sky

**Last updated:** August 2, 2026

Sidereal Sky ("the app," "we," "our") is developed by Valkyrie Agency LLC ("we," "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

## Summary

Sidereal Sky is designed with privacy as a priority. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to us or to any third party. Any personal information you enter is stored only on your own device.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
If you choose to enter birth information (such as date, time, and location of birth) to generate a natal chart, this information is stored **locally on your device only**. It is not transmitted to us, uploaded to any server we control, or shared with any third party.

### Information We Do Not Collect
Sidereal Sky does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking tools. We do not know who our users are, how often they use the app, or what data they enter.

## Third-Party Services

Sidereal Sky retrieves publicly available astronomical data (such as planetary positions) from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Horizons API in order to calculate sidereal planetary and lunar positions shown in the app. These requests contain only the astronomical parameters needed to retrieve ephemeris data (such as dates and celestial body identifiers). They do not include any personal information about you, such as your name, birth data, or device identifiers.

Use of the NASA JPL API is subject to NASA's own data usage terms. We do not control and are not responsible for NASA's data practices. You can review NASA JPL's policies directly at their website.

## Data Storage and Security

All personal data you choose to enter (such as birth information for chart calculations) is stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is not backed up to any server we operate. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Sidereal Sky is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Future Changes: Account Creation

We plan to introduce optional account creation in a future version of Sidereal Sky, which may involve collecting an email address and enabling data sync across devices. If and when this feature is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance to describe what information would be collected, how it would be stored and secured, and what choices you would have regarding that data. Continued use of the app after such an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Your Choices

Because all personal data is stored locally on your device, you can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
